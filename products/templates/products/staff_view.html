Staff Deactivation & Data Governance Policy

Under Indian GST and Enterprise Retail accounting standards, staff operator profiles cannot be hard-deleted from the database once a billing invoice or cashier receipt has been issued. Deactivating a staff member revokes POS workstation authentication privileges while indefinitely preserving historical sales ledger integrity and audit attribution trails.
